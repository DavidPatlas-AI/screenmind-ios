Trust by design

Your memories are yours.

Does the demo upload my data?

No. The public sample is built from fictional records; search runs in your browser. This page has no analytics, tracking, registration form or AI endpoint.

What about the native prototype?

Local processing is the default. An experimental external text-analysis option requires explicit preview and consent plus a privately configured gateway. It is not a deployed cloud service. Do not submit sensitive or classified information. Exported files may contain private data.

Is ScreenMind available commercially?

Not yet. ScreenMind is an early-stage prototype. No public App Store release, pricing, cloud hosting or customer traction is claimed on this website.
